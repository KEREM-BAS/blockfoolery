Blockfoolery makes short, original animated comedy clips in a blocky voxel style, published as YouTube Shorts, TikTok videos, Instagram Reels and Facebook Reels.

Blockfoolery Uploader

Blockfoolery Uploader is a private desktop tool run by the Blockfoolery team on its own computer. It only publishes Blockfoolery's own finished videos to Blockfoolery's own accounts:

  • YouTube: uploads videos to the Blockfoolery channel (YouTube Data API).
  • TikTok: sends videos to the Blockfoolery account's TikTok inbox as drafts; the account owner reviews and posts them in the TikTok app (Content Posting API, upload mode).
  • Instagram and Facebook: publishes Reels to the Blockfoolery Instagram account and Facebook Page (Graph API).

The tool has no other users, no public sign-up, and does not read or store anyone else's data.
